# BhoomiX — Round 2 Pitch Script

**4 speakers · 180 seconds maximum**

| Speaking pace | Runtime |
|---|---|
| Fast (160 wpm) | 2:22 |
| **Normal (145 wpm) — expect this** | **2:36** |
| Slow / nervous (130 wpm) | 2:54 |

Fits under 3:00 **even if you speak slowly on camera.** That headroom is
deliberate — nerves make people slower, not faster, and an over-length video
can be disqualified outright.

---

## ⚠️ Fill these in before filming

Five slots are marked `[LIKE THIS]`. They are your real user research and I
have deliberately **not** invented them. The masterclass says judges reward
honesty; a made-up farmer quote is the fastest way to lose the room if
anyone asks a follow-up question.

| Slot | What goes there |
|---|---|
| `[NAMES]` | Your four names |
| `[N]` farmers | How many you actually spoke to |
| `[M]` | How many confirmed the problem |
| `[QUOTE]` | One real sentence, their words |
| `[ONE CHANGE]` | One thing you changed because of them |
| `[GRANT PLAN]` | Specific and small — beats a big vague dream |

Not done the interviews? Do them today. Five farmers, fifteen minutes each.
The masterclass lists this as a scored requirement, and it is the one thing
in this script you cannot fake.

### 🎙️ SPEAKER 1 — *The Problem* · 0:00 – 0:38

> **ON SCREEN:** Two price cards side by side — Raisen ₹2,000 · Ujjain ₹2,701.
> The ₹701 gap animates between them.

**"Today, wheat sold for two thousand rupees a quintal in Raisen.**

**In Ujjain — two thousand seven hundred and one.**

**Same crop. Same day. Same state. Seven hundred rupees a quintal, gone. On
ten quintals, that's seven thousand rupees a farmer never sees — not from a
bad harvest, but because nobody told him.**

*(beat)*

**We're [NAMES], from Indore. This is BhoomiX.**

**A farmer here loses money three ways. He sells at the wrong mandi. He
misreads a disease until the field is gone. He misses the seventy-two-hour
window to claim his insurance."**

**Photograph a leaf. Our AI names the disease, gives a confidence score, and

**Crop damaged? Photos stamped with GPS and time, paired with recorded

**Twenty-three Indian languages."**

## Why it's built this way

**The first ten seconds are two numbers, not an introduction.** The
masterclass demands clarity in ten seconds. Names come *after* the hook.

**Every figure is verifiable.** ₹2,000 and ₹2,701 are real wheat rates from
Madhya Pradesh mandis, pulled from data.gov.in on 8 August 2026 — 100 markets
reporting. **Refresh them the morning you film:** open Mandi Prices, filter to
Wheat, take the lowest and highest, update the two numbers. A judge who checks
will find them true.

**Show a screen recording, not slides.** The masterclass rates a working
prototype above a polished one. Yours genuinely runs — most Round 2 entries
will be Figma mockups. Let that difference show.

**The close is the moat, not a feature list.** Judges fund defensibility.
Diagnosis → treatment → outcome is the one thing a larger competitor cannot
buy, so it earns the final 45 seconds.

**What's deliberately left out:** revenue model, tech stack, team CVs. At 180
seconds every sentence must earn its place, and none of those change whether
a judge believes you.
